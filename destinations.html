When to Travel

Curated Four-Season Travel Almanac

Match your holiday rhythm with the planet's most magnificent seasonal phenomena and optimal climatic windows.

Spring (Mar – May)
Kyoto & Amalfi Bloom

Peak Sakura blossoms across Kyoto's historic canals and lemon grove blooms along Italy's southern cliffside towns.

  • Avg Temp: 19°C – 23°C
  • Ideal for: Cultural walking
  • Crowd Index: Moderate
Summer (Jun – Aug)
Santorini & Bali Sun

Dry, sunny trade winds in Bali and endless golden caldera sunsets with private catamaran anchorages across Greece.

  • Avg Temp: 28°C – 32°C
  • Ideal for: Yachting & diving
  • Crowd Index: Vibrant
Autumn (Sep – Nov)
Swiss Harvest & Serengeti

Crisp alpine hiking beneath golden larches in Switzerland and dramatic predator encounters along the Mara River migration.

  • Avg Temp: 15°C – 22°C
  • Ideal for: Wildlife & wine
  • Crowd Index: Peaceful
Winter (Dec – Feb)
Maldives & Swiss Alps

Flawless powder snow across Zermatt's slopes, contrasted with glass-still lagoons and manta ray gatherings in the Maldives.

  • Avg Temp: -4°C or +30°C
  • Ideal for: Ski & reef diving
  • Crowd Index: Festive High
